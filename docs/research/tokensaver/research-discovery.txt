SUPERSEDED OR EXCLUDED CLAIMS
- About 43M replay-weighted ANSI characters in codex-review-0903 and
  codex-review-0903-after came from an outdated Python mirror. The September 4
  correction governs interpretation. Even its 22.6938M replacement is a
  hypothetical replay estimate, distinct from September 8 actual C# replay.
- claude-plan/curated_stages_mirror_20260816_100026.json through ...100029.json
  contain historical scope-expansion simulations with old mirror semantics.
- rerun_economics reports use heuristic classification and cost assumptions.
  No causal net-savings conclusion is drawn from them.
- An old file-read character percentage mixed denominators; this revision uses
  explicitly stated counts and denominators instead.
- Brotli archival compression ratios of about 7.4-16.7x describe disk storage,
  not TokenSaver request savings. Excluded from efficacy figures.
- custom_envs/claude_context_budget.md records schema/context deferral work.
  User scope is TokenSaver compression, so it is excluded from savings totals.
- A 222-turn prose token calibration was referenced, but no standalone saved
  calibration dataset was identified. It cannot calibrate all tool-output types.
- prompt-eval and root results concern Board prompting and Quality UI checks.

UNRESOLVED EVIDENCE GAP
No saved matched model-token counterfactual or randomized complete-task quality
comparison was found in this search. The paper supplies conditional proofs and
measured transport reductions, then specifies an experiment for net task cost
and quality. The search does not justify a claim of measured billed-token savings
or universal preservation of useful information.
