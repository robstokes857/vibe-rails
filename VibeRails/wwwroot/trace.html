Contract

`ITerminalIoObserver`

Only the observer-driven event families belong here.

OnTerminalIoAsync

Dispatches `TerminalIoEvent` and becomes `TerminalInput` or `TerminalOutput` in the trace buffer.

OnTerminalResizeAsync

Fires when PTY dimensions change through the terminal state service and becomes `Resize`.

OnTerminalIdleAsync

Fires after the idle monitor crosses the threshold and becomes `Idle`.

OnTerminalRemoteCommandAsync

Fires for remote browser commands and lands in the trace as `SessionLifecycle` with a remote-command summary.

`TerminalLaunch` is intentionally not shown here. It is emitted directly by `TerminalRunner`, not by the `ITerminalIoObserver` pipeline.
